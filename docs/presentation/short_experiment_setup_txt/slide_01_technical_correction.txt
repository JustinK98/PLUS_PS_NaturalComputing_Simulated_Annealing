Slide 1: Technical correction

The experiment does not evaluate a changed activation layout by retraining it from scratch at every step.

Instead, Online-Delta-SA compares two layouts under controlled conditions:

- same network weights
- same mini-batch
- only one hidden-neuron activation function changed
- reduction: only set_neuron as AF change 
- no candidate training before the acceptance decision

This makes the measured loss difference an immediate effect of the activation-function change, not an effect of additional training.
