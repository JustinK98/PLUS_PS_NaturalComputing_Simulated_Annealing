Slide 6: Open questions

Questions we can discuss:

- Does Online-Delta-SA improve over the matched random baseline?

- Is the improvement caused by the found layout, or mainly by the inherited training path?

- Do the best layouts show similar activation-function patterns?

- Which activation functions occur most often in good layouts?

- Are the SA parameters numerically healthy?
  For example: acceptance rate, temperature schedule, and number of steps.

- Does the method behave similarly across all benchmarks,
  or only on easier benchmarks such as two_moons?

- For difficult benchmarks, is the limitation caused by SA,
  the optimizer, the fixed topology, or the benchmark itself?
